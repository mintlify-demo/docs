## Support hours

The support inbox is staffed **Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm Pacific**.

## On-call

Starting **December 1**, one agent covers the weekend on-call: **Saturday and Sunday, 10am to 4pm Pacific**. The on-call agent rotates weekly.

The on-call agent is the first stop for escalations in #support-escalations. See [Escalation process](/playbook/escalation-process).

## Time off

Tell the support lead about time off at least **2 weeks** ahead so the team can cover your shifts.

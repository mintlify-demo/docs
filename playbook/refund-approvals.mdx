Customers can ask for a refund within 30 days of their order. See the public [Refunds](/acme/refunds) page for what they see.

| Refund amount | Who approves |
| --- | --- |
| Up to $50 | Any support agent |
| $50 to $200 | Support lead |
| Over $200 | Head of customer experience |

## Before you approve

- Check the order is less than 30 days old.
- Check the customer hasn't had more than 2 refunds this year.
- Add a note to the ticket with the reason.

<Warning>Never refund to a different card than the one used for the order.</Warning>

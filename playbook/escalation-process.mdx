<Info>This process takes effect on **December 1**.</Info>

Escalate a ticket when you can't solve it yourself within the time below.

| Priority | Example | Escalate after |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Urgent | Customer charged twice, order lost | **1 hour** |
| High | Order late by more than 5 days | **4 hours** |
| Normal | Product question, account change | **3 business days** |

## How to escalate

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tag the ticket">
    Add the `escalated` tag and set the priority.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Post in #support-escalations">
    Paste the ticket link and one sentence on what you tried. The on-call agent picks it up first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="On-call agent loops in the support lead if needed">
    The on-call agent loops in the support lead only if they can't solve it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>Escalations go to the **on-call agent** in #support-escalations first. On weekends, the weekend on-call agent covers Saturday and Sunday, 10am to 4pm Pacific. See [Hours and on-call](/playbook/hours-and-on-call).</Note>

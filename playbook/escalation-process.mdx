Escalate a ticket when you can't solve it yourself within the time below.

| Priority | Example | Escalate after |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Urgent | Customer charged twice, order lost | **4 hours** |
| High | Order late by more than 5 days | **1 business day** |
| Normal | Product question, account change | **3 business days** |

## How to escalate

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tag the ticket">
    Add the `escalated` tag and set the priority.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Post in #support-escalations">
    Paste the ticket link and one sentence on what you tried.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign to the support lead">
    The support lead picks it up or hands it to the right team.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>Escalations go to the **support lead** during support hours. Outside hours, they wait until the next business day.</Note>

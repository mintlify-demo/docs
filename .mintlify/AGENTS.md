# Acme docs: writing rules

## Voice
- Write in second person ("you") and active voice.
- Keep sentences under 25 words.

## Terms
- Say "Acme Coffee Club", never "the club" or "ACC".
- Say "sign in", never "log in" or "login" as a verb.
- Don't use: utilize, simply, just, easy.

## Page templates
- How-to pages: one-line intro, numbered steps, then "Next steps".
- Every API page shows a request and a response example.

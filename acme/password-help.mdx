## Choosing a password

Your password must be at least **8 characters** long.

<Tip>A short sentence makes a strong password that's easy to remember, like `my dog loves coffee`.</Tip>

## Forgot your password?

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the log-in page">
    <Tabs>
      <Tab title="Website" icon="globe">
        Go to **acmecoffee.club** and click **Log in**.
      </Tab>
      <Tab title="App" icon="mobile-screen">
        Open the Acme app and tap **Log in**.
      </Tab>
    </Tabs>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Forgot password">
    Enter the email address you signed up with.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your email">
    We send you a link. Click it and choose a new password.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>The reset link only works once. If it doesn't arrive in a few minutes, check your spam folder.</Note>

Every new member gets a **14-day free trial**. You won't pay anything during your trial.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up">
    [Create your account](/acme/getting-started). Your trial starts as soon as you click **Sign up**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enjoy your coffee">
    Try Acme for 14 days, free.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep going or cancel">
    When your trial ends, your plan starts. If you don't want to continue, cancel any time before the trial ends and you won't be charged.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>Want to know what happens after your trial? See [Plans & pricing](/acme/plans-and-pricing).</Tip>

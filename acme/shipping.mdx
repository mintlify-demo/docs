## Send one delivery to a different address

You can send a single delivery to a different address, for example while you're traveling. Later deliveries go back to your usual address.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find your next delivery">
    Open **Account**, then **Deliveries**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Change address">
    Click **Change address** next to your next delivery and enter the new address. The new address is used for that one delivery only.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>Make the change at least **2 days** before the ship date. The new address must be in the United States or Mexico.</Note>

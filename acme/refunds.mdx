Not happy with an order? You can ask for a refund within **30 days** of your order.

## How to request a refund

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find your order">
    Open **Account**, then **Orders**, and find the order you want refunded.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Request a refund">
    Click **Request a refund** next to the order.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tell us why">
    Pick a reason from the list. This step is optional, but it helps us improve.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get your money back">
    We send your money back to the card you paid with.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>Orders older than 30 days can't be refunded.</Warning>

## Charged twice or charged the wrong amount

If you see a duplicate or incorrect charge for your plan, open **Account**, then **Orders**, and request a refund on the extra charge using the steps above.

If the charge doesn't appear under **Orders**, or the refund doesn't go through, [contact us](/acme/contact-us). Include the date and amount of the charge so our support team can find it.

Need help with a refund? [Contact us](/acme/contact-us).

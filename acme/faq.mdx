<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How much does Acme cost?" icon="tags">
    It depends on your plan. Free is $0, Pro is $15 per month, and Team is $29 per month per person. See [Plans & pricing](/acme/plans-and-pricing).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is there a free trial?" icon="gift">
    Yes. Every new member gets a 30-day free trial. See [Free trial](/acme/free-trial).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where do you ship?" icon="truck">
    We ship to the United States, Canada, and Mexico. See [Shipping](/acme/shipping).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I get a refund?" icon="rotate-left">
    Yes, within 60 days of your order. See [Refunds](/acme/refunds).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="When can I talk to someone?" icon="headset">
    Our support team is available Monday-Friday, 8am-8pm Pacific. See [Contact us](/acme/contact-us).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is there dark mode?" icon="moon">
    Yes. Turn it on in **Settings** > **Appearance**. <Badge color="green" icon="check">Available</Badge>
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I cancel any time?" icon="xmark">
    Yes. You can cancel your plan whenever you like from **Account** settings.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I change my email address?" icon="envelope">
    Go to **Account** settings, click **Email**, type your new address, and click **Save**. We send a confirmation link to your new address. Click it to finish the change.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

Still stuck? [Contact us](/acme/contact-us).
